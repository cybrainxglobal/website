Master Your Workflow

Axiom is an all-in-one productivity app. Type or say what you need and the AI files it as a task, an alarm or a quick note — then plan it on a list, a Kanban board, a Gantt timeline or the calendar, and follow your progress on the dashboard.

Key Features

AI Input

Type or speak: the AI decides whether it is a task, an alarm or a quick note and fills in the date, time and subtasks.

Kanban Boards

Move tasks through Backlog, To Do, In Progress and Completed at a glance.

Gantt Charts

See your plan on a visual timeline with progress bars.

Calendar

See tasks by day and reschedule them with a long press.

Alarms & Reminders

Recurring tasks and exact alarms that ring at the time you set.

Progress Dashboard

Completion rate, weekly trend and streaks to keep your momentum.
