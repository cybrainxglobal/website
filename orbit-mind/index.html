From the first thought to the final presentation

A workspace that follows your thinking.

01

Start without a connection

Create and edit maps on your device. Your local library works without an account or an internet connection.

02

Make the structure yours

Arrange topics, choose layouts and colors, add notes and personalized tooltips, and move around your map freely.

03

Give AI a starting point

Describe a subject and review an AI-generated draft before adding it to your library. Sign-in is required, and your account's credit limits apply.

04

Keep the context together

Add images, files and audio to topics. Cloud uploads follow your account's storage quota.

05

Pick up on another device

Sign in with Google or a verified email and password to synchronize opened maps between Android and the web.

06

Show the bigger picture

Present your map, export it, or create a read-only link. Anyone with an active link can view the map; you can stop sharing at any time.

Clear choices, from the start

Know where your ideas go.

Local maps stay on your device. Signing in enables cloud synchronization; AI requests are sent to the providers that process them. Read the details of account access, attachments, advertising and retention before choosing the online features.
