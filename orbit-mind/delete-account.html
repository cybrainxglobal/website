Delete your account or data.

You can request deletion without installing or reinstalling Orbit Mind.

Delete your Orbit Mind account

  1. Open the account deletion page below.
  2. Sign in to the Orbit Mind account you want to delete.
  3. Enter your account email to confirm. If you use email/password sign-in, confirm your Orbit Mind password too.
  4. Choose “Permanently delete my account”. Export any maps you want to keep before you confirm.
Open account deletion ↗

In the Android app, you can also use Settings → Account → Delete account.

What account deletion removes

The request covers your account, cloud maps, attachments, shared links and feedback. Sessions end immediately. Server cleanup runs in the background and can take several hours. Backup copies expire according to the retention policy.

Local copies on your devices are separate. Clear the app or browser's stored data to remove them. People who previously downloaded a shared map may retain their own copy.

For the retention periods and data handled by service providers, read the Orbit Mind Privacy Policy.

Keep your account and request data deletion

Email oberdan@cybrainx.com from the address associated with your Orbit Mind account. Specify which data you want deleted and whether you want to keep the account. Requests are reviewed manually, and we may ask you to verify ownership.

Do not include your password, sign-in code or payment details. If you cannot access the account, contact the same support address for assistance. We respond within the applicable legal time limits.

Excluir conta ou dados do Orbit Mind

Você pode solicitar a exclusão sem reinstalar o aplicativo. Abra a página de exclusão da conta, entre na conta correta, confirme o e-mail e, quando solicitado, sua senha do Orbit Mind. Exporte antes os mapas que deseja guardar.

Para excluir apenas determinados dados e manter a conta, ou se você não conseguir entrar, escreva para oberdan@cybrainx.com informando quais dados deseja excluir. O atendimento é manual e pode exigir confirmação de titularidade. Não envie senhas ou códigos.

A exclusão encerra as sessões imediatamente; a limpeza da nuvem ocorre em segundo plano e pode levar algumas horas. Cópias locais devem ser removidas no próprio aparelho ou navegador. Consulte os detalhes na Política de Privacidade.
